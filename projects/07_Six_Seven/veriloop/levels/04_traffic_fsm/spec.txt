Module name: traffic_light
Ports: input clk, input reset, input ped, output reg [1:0] light, output walk
Encoding of light: 2'b00 = RED, 2'b01 = GREEN, 2'b10 = YELLOW.
Everything changes on the rising edge of clk. reset is synchronous and has priority.
- On a rising edge with reset = 1, light becomes RED. That cycle is the first of RED's cycles.
- Without a pedestrian, the light repeats: RED for 3 cycles, then GREEN for 4 cycles, then YELLOW for
  2 cycles, then RED again.
- ped is a pedestrian button. On a rising edge where light is GREEN and ped = 1, light becomes YELLOW
  (green ends early); YELLOW then lasts its full 2 cycles. ped is ignored while light is YELLOW or RED.
- walk is 1 exactly when light is RED, otherwise 0.
