# Attempt log: Qwen3-8B batch-1 decode megakernel on Trainium2

Every kernel attempt in the order it was made, with what the checker said and what changed next.
All on one trn2 seat pod, one logical NeuronCore (LNC=2), random weights at Qwen3-8B shapes for
speed, and real Qwen3-8B weights for correctness. Raw rows are in `results/*.jsonl` and `results/*.log`;
`results/check_log.jsonl` holds every run of `check.py`.

**Final kernel:** `qwen3_decode_step_resident` (attempts 2 + 4 + 8 + 11, generate loop from 14). Accepted by
`check.py --resident` on all five gates. 27.74 ms per token on the device (3 runs: 27.71–27.79) vs 47.50 per-op;
27.55 ms end to end; teacher-forced 94/96 steps on 3 prompts, both misses explained by bf16 noise.

Scores: **correctness** = rel-L2 of the hidden state or logits against HuggingFace float32, or argmax
agreement; **speed** = ms per decode step, one launch, synced every call, median of 30 (100 where noted).
